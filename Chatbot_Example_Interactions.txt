----------------------------------------------------------------
                        INTRODUCTION
----------------------------------------------------------------

This document presents example conversations between a user and
the Health & Fitness Chatbot. The chatbot uses rule-based
if-else conditions to detect keywords in user input and respond
with relevant health and fitness information.

The chatbot covers the following topics:
  1.  Greetings
  2.  Workout / Exercise
  3.  Weight Loss
  4.  Weight / Muscle Gain
  5.  Diet & Nutrition
  6.  Protein & Supplements
  7.  Hydration / Water Intake
  8.  Sleep & Recovery
  9.  Cardio
  10. Stretching / Flexibility
  11. Mental Health & Fitness
  12. BMI (Body Mass Index)
  13. Farewell

----------------------------------------------------------------
                    HOW THE CHATBOT WORKS
----------------------------------------------------------------

The chatbot reads the user's input, converts it to lowercase,
and checks it against a series of if-elif-else conditions. Each
condition looks for specific keywords or phrases. When a match
is found, the corresponding response is returned. If no keyword
matches, a fallback message is displayed.

Key design decisions:
  - Input is normalised to lowercase for case-insensitive matching.
  - The 'any()' function checks for multiple keyword variants.
  - Nested conditions handle more specific sub-topics.
  - A while loop keeps the chat running until the user exits.

         Type 'help' to see what I can answer.

Chatbot: Here are the topics I can help you with:
           1. Workout / Exercise
           2. Weight Loss / Gain
           3. Diet / Nutrition
           4. Protein & Supplements
           5. Hydration / Water Intake
           6. Sleep & Recovery
           7. Cardio
           8. Stretching / Flexibility
           9. Mental Health & Fitness
          10. BMI
         Just type a question or keyword!

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 2 — Workout for Beginners
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: I am new to working out, how do I start?

Chatbot: For beginners, start with 3 days a week of full-body workouts.
         Focus on compound movements: squats, push-ups, lunges, and rows.
         Rest at least one day between sessions to allow muscle recovery.

---

You: how many days per week should I exercise?

Chatbot: Most people benefit from working out 3–5 days per week.
         Beginners: 3 days | Intermediate: 4 days | Advanced: 5–6 days.
         Always include at least 1–2 rest days for recovery.

---

You: what are good home workout exercises?

Chatbot: Great home workout exercises include:
           • Push-ups (chest & triceps)
           • Squats and lunges (legs)
           • Plank (core)
           • Mountain climbers (cardio + core)
           • Glute bridges (glutes & hamstrings)
         No equipment needed!

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 3 — Weight Loss
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: how do I lose weight?

Chatbot: Key principles of weight loss:
           1. Caloric deficit — burn more calories than you consume.
           2. Eat whole foods: vegetables, lean protein, whole grains.
           3. Do both cardio AND strength training.
           4. Sleep 7–9 hours; poor sleep raises hunger hormones.
           5. Stay hydrated — sometimes thirst feels like hunger.
         Aim to lose 0.5–1 kg per week for sustainable results.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 4 — Building Muscle
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: I want to build muscle and gain weight

Chatbot: To gain weight / build muscle:
           1. Eat in a caloric surplus (300–500 extra calories/day).
           2. Prioritise protein: 1.6–2.2 g per kg of body weight.
           3. Lift heavy with progressive overload.
           4. Compound lifts: bench press, deadlift, squat, overhead press.
           5. Rest adequately — muscles grow during recovery, not in the gym.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 5 — Diet & Nutrition
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: what are the best foods to eat?

Chatbot: Focus on nutrient-dense foods:
           ✅ Lean proteins: chicken, fish, eggs, legumes
           ✅ Complex carbs: oats, brown rice, sweet potato
           ✅ Healthy fats: avocado, nuts, olive oil
           ✅ Vegetables & fruits (fibre + micronutrients)
           ❌ Limit: processed foods, sugary drinks, trans fats

---

You: how many calories should I eat daily?

Chatbot: Daily calorie needs vary by goal:
           • Weight loss  : 1,500–1,800 kcal (average adult)
           • Maintenance  : 2,000–2,200 kcal
           • Weight gain  : 2,500–3,000 kcal
         Use a TDEE calculator for a personalised number.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 6 — Protein & Supplements
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: how much protein do I need daily?

Chatbot: Recommended daily protein intake:
           • Sedentary adults  : 0.8 g / kg body weight
           • Active individuals: 1.2–1.6 g / kg body weight
           • Athletes / lifters: 1.6–2.2 g / kg body weight
         Good sources: eggs, chicken, fish, tofu, Greek yogurt, lentils.

---

You: tell me about creatine

Chatbot: Creatine monohydrate is one of the most researched supplements.
           • Take 3–5 g per day consistently.
           • Helps increase strength and power output.
           • Safe for most healthy adults.
           • Stay well-hydrated while using it.

---

You: what is whey protein?

Chatbot: Whey protein is a fast-digesting protein ideal post-workout.
           • 20–30 g post-workout helps muscle recovery.
           • Not essential if you meet protein needs through food.
           • Alternatives: pea protein, soy protein (for vegans).

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 7 — Hydration
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: how much water should I drink?

Chatbot: Hydration guidelines:
           • General rule : ~8 glasses (2 litres) per day.
           • Active people: 3–4 litres, more in hot climates.
           • Drink water before, during, and after workouts.
           • Signs of dehydration: dark urine, fatigue, headache.
           • Tip: carry a water bottle as a constant reminder!

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 8 — Sleep & Recovery
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: how many hours of sleep do I need?

Chatbot: Adults need 7–9 hours of sleep per night.
         Athletes or heavy trainers may benefit from up to 10 hours.
         Sleep is when your muscles repair and grow — never skip rest!

---

You: what are good recovery tips?

Chatbot: Recovery tips:
           • Sleep 7–9 hours every night.
           • Take 1–2 rest days per week from intense training.
           • Use active recovery: light walking, yoga, swimming.
           • Eat protein after workouts to aid muscle repair.
           • Stretch and foam-roll to reduce soreness.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 9 — Cardio
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: what is HIIT?

Chatbot: HIIT (High-Intensity Interval Training):
           • Alternates short bursts of max effort with brief rest.
           • Example: 30 sec sprint → 30 sec walk × 10 rounds.
           • Burns more calories in less time than steady-state cardio.
           • Do 2–3 sessions per week; avoid consecutive days.

---

You: how long should I do cardio?

Chatbot: Cardio duration recommendations:
           • Minimum: 150 min moderate OR 75 min vigorous per week.
           • For weight loss: aim for 200–300 min moderate per week.
           • Split into 30–60 min sessions for manageability.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 10 — Stretching & Flexibility
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: should I stretch before or after workout?

Chatbot: Stretching & flexibility tips:
           • Warm up with 5–10 min of light cardio before workouts.
           • Cool down with static stretches (hold 20–30 sec each).
           • Yoga or mobility work 2–3× per week improves range of motion.
           • Key muscles to stretch: hamstrings, hip flexors, chest, shoulders.
           • Never bounce during static stretches — risk of injury.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 11 — Mental Health
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: I feel stressed and have no motivation to exercise

Chatbot: Exercise and mental health:
           • Just 30 min of moderate exercise releases endorphins.
           • Regular activity reduces symptoms of anxiety and depression.
           • Set small, achievable goals to build confidence and motivation.
           • Try mindfulness or meditation alongside your fitness routine.
           • If struggling, speak to a healthcare professional — fitness
             is one part of overall wellness.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 12 — BMI
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: what is a healthy BMI?

Chatbot: BMI (Body Mass Index) categories:
           • Underweight : BMI < 18.5
           • Normal      : BMI 18.5 – 24.9
           • Overweight  : BMI 25 – 29.9
           • Obese       : BMI ≥ 30
         Formula: BMI = weight(kg) ÷ height(m)²
         Note: BMI doesn't account for muscle mass — use it as a guide only.

----------------------------------------------------------------

----------------------------------------------------------------

Chatbot: I'm not sure I understand that. 🤔
         Try asking about: workout, diet, weight loss, protein, cardio,
         sleep, hydration, stretching, mental health, or BMI.
         Type 'help' for the full topic list.

----------------------------------------------------------------
INTERACTION 14 — Goodbye
----------------------------------------------------------------

You: thanks, bye!

Chatbot: Thanks for chatting! Stay active, eat well, and rest enough. 🏃‍♂️
         Remember: small daily improvements lead to big results. Goodbye! 👋
